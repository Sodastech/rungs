Rungs is a library: your application calls it to send a record (an expense, a leave request, a
purchase order) through approval steps, and Rungs decides who must approve, keeps the state and
writes the audit trail. It runs in your Node.js process, on your database.

## Install

<Install />

Rungs needs Node.js 22 or newer and is published as ES modules with TypeScript types.
`@rungsjs/store-postgres` keeps everything in PostgreSQL 15 or newer; for tests and prototypes,
`@rungsjs/store-memory` keeps it in memory.

## Your first flow

An expense goes to the requester's manager, then to finance when it is above 1,000:

<Snippet file="quick-start.ts" region="quick-start" />

What happened:

- **`createRungs`** gets a store and the things Rungs never decides by itself: the time, how to
  make IDs, and how to find approvers. `roles` turns a role into users; `directManager` is a
  _resolver_ you write, here from a lookup table.
- **`flows.register`** stores a versioned flow. Each request keeps the version it started with.
- **`submit`** starts a request. Both steps apply because 2,500 is above 1,000, so Bob gets a
  task first.
- **`approve`** records each decision. Either finance user may decide, so Bob's and one finance
  approval complete the request.

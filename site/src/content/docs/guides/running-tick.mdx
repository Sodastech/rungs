Rungs has no background thread. Deadlines, reminders, escalations, timeouts and the reminders of
blocked steps are applied when you call `rungs.tick()`, at the time your `clock` returns. Run it
on a schedule:

<Snippet file="guides/tick.ts" region="worker" />

- **How often:** every minute is plenty for deadlines measured in hours or days.
- **Several workers:** safe. Each due action is applied once, and the PostgreSQL store hands
  different requests to different workers ([EC-27](/rungs/edge-cases/#ec-27)).
- **Missed runs:** a late tick catches up with one reminder, not one per missed interval.
- **Failures:** one failing request does not stop the others; it is reported in `failed` and
  tried again next time.

On serverless platforms, call the same two functions from a scheduled function or cron route
instead of `setInterval`.

Rungs never deletes data on its own. How long you keep approvals, and how you honor requests to
erase personal data (for example under GDPR or Indonesia's UU PDP), is your policy; the PostgreSQL
store gives you the functions to carry it out ([EC-61](/rungs/edge-cases/#ec-61)).

<Snippet file="guides/retention.ts" region="policy" />

`purgeRequests` deletes closed requests (approved, rejected or withdrawn) with their audit trail,
outbox events, inbox rows and idempotency records, in one transaction, at most `limit` (default
1,000) per call. The tenant scope is always explicit: a forgotten option never deletes another
tenant's data.

<Snippet file="guides/retention.ts" region="erase" />

## Housekeeping

<Snippet file="guides/retention.ts" region="housekeeping" />

After `purgeIdempotency`, repeating one of those commands runs it again, so keep keys at least as
long as clients may retry. `purgeDispatchedEvents` never touches undelivered events.

Outside these functions, audit rows cannot be updated or deleted: a database trigger refuses it.
Data your application copied elsewhere (emails, logs, your own tables) is yours to delete.

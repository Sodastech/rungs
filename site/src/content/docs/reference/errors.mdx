Every error extends `RungsError` and has a stable `code`. Branch on the class or the code, never on
the message, which may become more helpful over time.

<DesignDoc section="Errors" show="table" />

Rungs checks workflow routing (is this actor assigned to a task?), not authentication or
permissions. Your application signs people in and decides, for example, who is an administrator,
before it calls a command.

`ResolutionError` is usually worth retrying: a callback failed and nothing changed.
`NoApproversError` is not: a step has nobody who can approve it until your data or the flow
changes.
